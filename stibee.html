01 Overview

Designing the Email Marketing Experience

Stibee is an email marketing platform for marketers and creators. As an early product designer, I worked across the product experience — from the newsletter editor and templates to the design system.

But helping customers succeed with email marketing went beyond the product itself. Together with the team, we helped customers create better email marketing through templates, education, inspiration and feedback.

02 Customer Experience

Beyond the Editor

Customers needed more than a tool to send emails. They needed to know what to create, how to create it, and what good email marketing looks like.

  • Templates
    Templates gave customers practical starting points for different types of email marketing, reducing the blank-page problem while showing what a good campaign could look like.
  • Inspiration
    Showing what good looks like.
  • Education
    Working with the team, we ran monthly seminars covering email marketing trends, best practices, and how to get better results with Stibee. We also provided hands-on design feedback and guidance, helping customers improve their campaigns in practice.
03 Customer Feedback

From customers back into the product

The monthly seminars were also a direct source of customer feedback. We learned about their challenges and the features they needed for their campaigns — helping the team shape the product roadmap around real customer needs.

Customer feedback → Design / Product → Education → Customer
Email Marketing Report

Every year, we surveyed marketers about their email marketing practices and published the findings as an industry report. I designed the report and its visual communication, turning customer and industry data into a resource for marketers.

04 Product foundation

Designing the product foundation

I led the end-to-end design across Stibee — from the dashboard, newsletter editor and creation flow to visual assets and marketing materials.

Flow

Front-loaded decisions, focused creation
Moved operational decisions earlier, so customers could focus on creating their content.

Editor

WYSIWYG editing, built for responsive emails
Made it easy to edit content and layout directly, while enabling customers to quickly create responsive emails across devices.

05 Key takeaway

Good product design is not only about making a tool easier to use. It’s about understanding what customers are trying to achieve — and designing the experience around that goal.
