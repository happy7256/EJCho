01 Brief

The Challenge of Complexity

Loop11 is a pioneer in usability testing, but as the platform grew, its core workflows — creating tests and reviewing results — became fragmented. Users were overwhelmed by UI noise, and critical actions were often buried.

I led the end-to-end redesign to transform Loop11 into a clearer, more structured platform for researchers.

02 Discover

Benchmarking the Friction

Before diving into design, we initiated a discovery phase to understand the "why" behind user frustration.

17.7%
of users cited the project list and reports UI as their primary pain point
32%
Only 32% of participants could successfully find the "Launch URL" in baseline usability testing

Through Intercom data analysis and interviews with power users, we identified the project list and reporting UI as the primary pain point. A baseline usability study confirmed this wasn't just aesthetic — only 32% of participants could complete a critical task. This was a fundamental usability failure.

“Couldn’t figure out how to delete or edit.”
— Customer interview
03
MANAGE Project List
Problem
Difficult to manage the projects

Users struggled to understand how to take action on their projects and find their current project status.

1. Not obvious
The cogwheel doesn’t clearly communicate edit/delete actions.
2. Unclear next step
It’s hard to know what action to take (edit, continue, or launch).
3. Hard to find status
Understanding the current state requires extra clicks.
Solution
A Unified Control Centre
  • Consolidated View
    Combined all project states into a single list using intuitive status tags — no more tab-switching to find what's live versus in draft.
  • Direct Insights
    Surfaced key outcomes — success, failure, and participant videos — directly on the list view. Researchers could understand the value of their tests at a glance, without navigating deeper.
04
BUILD Project Builder
Problem
A Confusing Setup Journey

The legacy study setup journey was harder than it needed to be. Researchers had to move between separate screens to build and review tasks and questions, making it difficult to see how the pieces fit together. This became especially problematic for complex studies, where creating and managing multiple tasks made it harder to maintain an overview of the study.

Solution
Direct Manipulation & WYSIWYG
  • Logical Stages
    Reorganised the setup into three clear stages: Format → Tasks & Questions → Settings. Each stage had a single job.
  • WYSIWYG Editor
    Introduced a drag-and-drop builder, allowing users to visually structure their studies. This reduced reliance on hidden menus and made the "Launch" action impossible to miss.
05
ANALYZE Reporting
Problem
High-level Data, Limited Insight

Reporting focused on task-level success rates without showing the flow of individual questions. This made it difficult for researchers to identify where participants struggled and understand the results in context.

Solution
Contextual Results

The redesigned report shows the full study structure, with tasks and their related questions clearly grouped together. Researchers can move directly from task-level results to individual questions, making it easier to understand the context behind the data and uncover insights faster.

06 Design System

Building a Foundation for Growth

“A redesign is only as effective as its ability to scale.”

As a small team with limited design resources, I built a design system to make the new experience consistent, reusable and easier to extend. The system became the foundation for both the product UI and its implementation in React.

07 Outcome

Redesigning the end-to-end research experience

The redesign reimagined Loop11’s core research workflow — from creating and managing studies, to conducting moderated sessions and turning participant data into insights.

Together, these changes created a clearer, more connected experience across the researcher journey — from study setup to research insight.
